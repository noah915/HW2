Why I Enjoy Hiking

Hiking is a simple way to spend time outside, get exercise, and notice details that are easy to miss in everyday life. Every trail offers something different, from quiet forests to wide mountain views.

National parks protect many of these special places for people and wildlife. Visiting a park also gives hikers a chance to practice responsible recreation by following trail rules and leaving natural areas as they found them.

    What to Bring on a Hike

    A thoughtful packing list helps a hike stay comfortable and safe. The right gear depends on the trail length, weather, and season.

    • Water and a snack
    • A trail map or downloaded route
    • Comfortable shoes with good traction
    • Sun protection, such as sunscreen and a hat
    • A small first-aid kit
